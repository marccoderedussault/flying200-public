# Flying 200 -- Elevator Pitch

Track cycling sprints are won by thousandths of a second, but athletes have no way to know if they're distributing their power optimally across the effort. They finish, look at a stopwatch, and guess at what to change.

**Flying 200** is a mobile app that takes your real ride data -- from Strava or a FIT file -- and runs it through a physics simulation of a 250-meter velodrome. It auto-detects your sprint efforts, calculates the aerodynamic drag your performance implies, and then optimizes your pacing strategy to find the fastest possible time within your actual energy budget.

The result: a segment-by-segment plan telling you exactly where to push harder, where to back off, and when to transition from standing to seated -- backed by the physics, not guesswork. Athletes see time improvements quantified in milliseconds before they even get back on the bike.

**For elite track sprinters and their coaches**, Flying 200 turns post-ride data into pre-ride strategy, all from an iPhone trackside between efforts.
